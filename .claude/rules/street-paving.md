# Street paving and the destruction map

Part of the facade system (`facades.md`): the walk in front of the buildings.

- **European paving under one destruction map (owner, 2026-10-01: about 30% of the walk literally gone, in clusters with long good stretches between, worse under ruined buildings, the share easy to change).** `road_floor_wreck_map.gd` is the map: world-space simplex at `FREQUENCY` 0.07, calibrated to uniform through a 65-entry CDF, biased ±0.10 per building ruin tier, cut at `DESTROYED_SHARE` 0.30 (the live `share` is set by the `walk_wreck` console command). It sorts each point into GOOD, ROUGH, FRAGMENT or GONE (bands 0.25 and 0.10 outside the gone edge); crater weight is `smoothstep(share*.45, share*.85, e)`. `road_floor_wreck.gd` lays two columns of 0.5 m tiles by the curb and 0.25 m setts toward the buildings; each `Piece` state (FLAT, MISSING, TILTED, SUNK, FRAGMENT) comes from the zone, and FRAGMENT pieces are broken prisms (`road_floor_paving_mesh.gd` `add_prism`). GONE has no tiles and no curb. Owner, same day: rubble rare, the middle of a gone stretch almost bare, broken pieces part of one dirt floor. `road_floor_wreck_ground.gd` builds the soil heightfield (`add_grid`, `PAVING_SOIL` material). Owner, same day, on the first dirt floor: a worn path, not a floor of even stones, ramping to the road as if thousands walked and drove over it. The dirt is a foot path `DIRT_DROP` 0.02 under the walk top along the buildings; it ramps (`RAMP_SHARE` 0.6 of the width, clamped 0.8-2.5 m, smoothstep profile) down to `RAMP_FOOT` 0.15 under the top at the old curb line, weighted by `smoothstep(0, 0.10, e)` so it grows in from the gone edge. Hollows are `DIP_DEPTH` 0.03, and a skirt column at `inner - 0.02` drops to `SKIRT_DROP` 0.18 to close the step to the gutter. Its scatter is edge-weighted: shards at 10% × edge, flat stones at 1% + 3% × edge, rare single chunks. FRAGMENT pieces settle level with the dirt (`drop` up to 0.032), flush with it; paving chunks are 8% (10% for a missing piece in FRAGMENT), curb GONE chunks 12%, one each. `PIT_DEPTH` is 0.12, so the `Sidewalk*` bed box (collision only, top -0.18) pokes about 3 cm above the ramp foot near the curb; it is invisible and nothing walks there. Soil gets `packed` 1.0, `gravel` 1.0 (gravel only in noise patches) and `litter` 0.25 in the shader. `road_floor_wreck_curb.gd` lays 1 m granite blocks buried 4 cm; the zone drives missing and knocked blocks. The mesh helper emits with a winding oracle (Godot fronts are clockwise) and a 64 m visibility range. The skin is `paving_surface.gdshader` (`COLOR.r` wreck, `g` tone, `b` up; `wreck_dirt` darkens rough paving; glare fades 18-45 m). Facade rubble is about a third of what it was (WORN 1-2 per hole, BROKEN 2-4, GUTTED 3-6, stacks 10%), half-buried with tops at `DIRT_Y` -0.08 + half the size and bottoms at `RUBBLE_Y0` -0.56. Each RoadFloor stores metas `walk_gone_0/1` (gone m, length m); the smoke prints `walk destroyed share` and fails outside [0.12, 0.50]. Junctions and branches join the same world-space map; corner pads keep the old flat look (`sidewalk_mat`), and a floor with `sidewalk_material` set keeps the flat boxes. `corridor_segment._push_sidewalk_wreck` feeds each plan's `(z0, z1, ruin)` and `detail_seed`; `set_sidewalk_wreck` skips the rebuild when the data is identical.
