# Combat, damage, classes and boons

## Stat pipeline

Order matters and is fixed:

```
GameBalance floor (BASE_DAMAGE_PER_SHOT, BASE_FIRE_RATE)
  → ClassDefinition identity   (damage_mult, fire_rate_mult, mag, reload, pellets, spread, speed, size, bounces)
  → BoonTraits adds/mults      (GunStatsController._apply_traits: boons and street cards)
  → temporary StatModifiers    (stims)
  → clamps
```

`BoonTraits` holds two layers: permanent boon traits and a replaceable street overlay set by the active act card(s). `set_street_overlay` replaces wholesale; boss fights merge several cards' overlays first (`ActCardCombat`). A bullet leaves the gun with the resulting `damage_per_shot` and is never scaled again.

## Invariants

1. **One damage number, no damage types.** A bullet's damage is `GameBalance.BASE_DAMAGE_PER_SHOT * ClassDefinition.damage_mult`. Boons and street cards scale it through the `gun_damage_per_shot` trait, applied once in `GunStatsController`. Blasts and poison are shares of the hit that caused them and are never scaled again.
2. **Shotgun pellets split damage**, they don't multiply it. An 8-pellet shotgun is coverage, not ×8 DPS.
3. **Fire, poison and cold are bullet boons, not damage types.** Explosive, Poison and Cold Rounds each have one `BoonBehavior` handler (`scripts/player/boon_behavior_handlers.gd`). Secondary damage (blast splash, poison ticks) never triggers them; a blast does apply poison and cold. Explosive bullets detonate on first contact and don't ricochet.
4. **"Reload Speed %" lowers duration:** `seconds / (1 + pct/100)`. Never add the percentage onto the seconds field.
5. **One gun per class.** The class is picked at the class board in IDLE and locked for the run. Keys 1–4 are tool slots; Q is bound but unused.
6. **No hitscan gun.** Combat is projectile-only.

## Adding content

- A boon or item: a `.tres` in `resources/items/` plus a pool entry, read by `ItemPoolRegistry` / `ItemRegistry`. Boon `.tres` files and boon pools are generated by `tools/generate_boons.py` (icons: `tools/generate_boon_icons.py`); change the generator and re-run it, never hand-edit its output.
- A class: a `.tres` in `resources/classes/`, found by `ClassCatalog` (folder scan).
- New behaviour is a new small `Resource` subclass (`ItemEffect`, or a `BoonBehavior` registered in `BoonBehaviorRegistry`), not a branch inside an existing system.
- Double Damage lives in the `warehouse_rare` pool: only the warehouse chest reads it; rests and the mechanic never do.

## Audio from combat

Don't call `play()` from `gun_controller`; emit `shot` and let `AudioDirector` map it to a `SoundCue`. See `audio.md`.
