# hands-blender-review research 02: knuckle width vs the wrist/palm floor (D23)

Question (owner delegated, 2026-10-18): T8 = 0.108 kept `wrist/palm` >= 0.70 but left the knuckle row under today's W5 bulge (.115), against D11 ("W7/W8 above today's W5"). Which wins for "our muscular creepy hand"?

## Anatomy
- Hand breadth (ANSUR definition: metacarpale II to V, palm on table) vs wrist breadth. Colombian men, n = 1315, 50th percentile: hand 8.45 cm, wrist 5.58 cm, so wrist/hand = 0.66 (PMC10308009 table 1). A plain human hand sits about 0.64..0.70.
- No muscle crosses the wrist, only tendons over bone; the palm's breadth is thenar and hypothenar mass, the interossei between the metacarpals and the MCP heads. A strong, worked hand grows at the palm and knuckle row, not at the wrist, so its ratio falls below the plain 0.66.
- The widest line of the back of a hand is the MCP row (the knuckle heads stand past the proximal phalanges), which is D11.

## Creature reading
- Gnarled, creepy hands (goblin, witch, ghoul designs) read through a narrow bony wrist under a broad knobbed knuckle row and long crooked fingers; a tube-like wrist as wide as the palm reads as a mitten or a glove.
- Today's 0.79 L / 0.82 R is that tube. The 0.70 floor sat at the top of the human range, so it forbade the very taper that reads muscular.
- The low end still needs a floor: under about 0.60 the palm reads as a balloon on a stick (Pass test 1, "no balloon"); wrist_step (<= 0.05) keeps the joint itself free of a collar or notch, and the 1.5-power ramp is steepest at the wrist, so the flare starts at once.

## Choice
- D11 wins. T8 = 0.120: W7 .118 / W8 .120 clear .115 by more than the 0.002 check; `wrist/palm` .677 R / .653 L (palm half 0.983 W8; fore r .0798 / .0770, fixed by Scope); jump 1.33 (independent of T8).
- Band 0.62..0.85: 0.62 = the plain-human 0.66 less the palm growth a muscular hand adds, above the balloon line; the 0.85 ceiling stays.
- Fallback: under 0.62, T8 = L fore r / (0.64 x measured palm half / W8).
