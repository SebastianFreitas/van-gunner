Background: van-gunner is a Godot 4.7 first-person roguelite with procedural goblin monster hands (scripts/player/arms/). On 2026-10-16 a geometry-only review found the hands read as a low-quality asset because of uniformity (four identical 8-sided finger tubes, one profile, one crook, one claw) and discontinuity (claw, finger, palm and forearm at different densities and scales, butted together: hard wrist collar, no knuckle row, stepped telescoping joints). The full review with file:line anchors and a fix order is at C:/Users/Traff/AppData/Local/Temp/vg-hands-review/hands-geometry-review.md; the look notes and 29 close-ups are next to it. Owner's standing rules: the posture is untouchable (never touch pose constants in arms_builder.gd or the pose layers), one fixed hand design with no seeded variation (fixed constants only), improve existing elements rather than remove them, keep the overall size and thickness, keep low-poly and a bit silly, the finger claws keep their full visible length (the owner rejected a pass where nails got swallowed into the tip). Textures and shaders are out of scope.

The plan's phases, in order of visible gain, each judged on 4x crops from `py -3 tools/hand_shots.py` against before shots taken on this branch before any change:
1. Per-digit tables in arm_fingers.gd (fixed constants, no rng): per finger a length scale, radius scale, crook amount and direction applied as a bend at a joint rather than a tip shear, and claw length; index and ring about 0.9 of the middle, little about 0.75, each with its own set.
2. Joint rhythm: knuckle knobs 1.3 to 1.5x the shaft radius, taper nearer 1 : 0.8 : 0.6 root to tip, a continuous profile across each joint instead of a step, two or three extra rings at each joint, a rounder cross-section (10 to 12 sides or a rounded square instead of the squashed octagon).
3. Knuckle row and web: metacarpal heads bulging on the back of the hand (arm_knuckles.gd) and a small web between finger roots (arm_finger_join.gd).
4. Wrist: taper the forearm into the hand and blend ArmBulk's gain across the seam so hand and forearm are one surface (arm_bulk.gd, arms_builder.gd finger girth vs palm gain).
5. Density: bring the claw mesh (arm_claw.gd, about 1,700 tris) to the finger's density, add rings to the forearm profile.
6. Claw root on the dorsal side of the tip and narrower than the tip, full length kept; judge the nails on a 4x fingertip crop against the before shot and treat "looks shorter" as a failure.

Constraints: never change pose constants or the pose layers; no seeded or per-van variation anywhere in hand shape; keep overall hand size and thickness; nothing covers or shortens the finger claws; low-poly stays. Every phase runs `py -3 tools/check.py` and `py -3 tools/smoke.py`, and `py -3 tools/smoke.py --shots` for the look; `--bless` only where the fingerprint is meant to change, said in the report.
