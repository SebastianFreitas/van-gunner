## Constraints (every phase)

- **One static model** for the single van: no seeded or procedural variety; the sandbox seed 1337 is the only seed any view uses (D2).
- **Construction** (D4): every floor piece is a closed SurfaceTool mesh built in a `RefCounted` helper and added under `VanFloor` (`Interior/Shell/Floor`) with layer 2 set before `add_child` (van-render-layers.md); no scene nodes, no seeded rolls. FLICKER (van-geometry.md "Seams"): a face never sits within 1 cm of a parallel face of another node that looks the same way and overlaps it over 1 cm²; opposite-facing faces may touch (a box on its host, a slab against the wall); pieces of one node at one height never overlap : butt them with a 1 cm gap. EDGE: no open edge, so host sheets carry their pockets and the well in their own mesh. Only phase 1 adds collision.
- **The proud-piece recipe** (D19, D20): a plate, bead, lip, nosing, mat or tread is a box sunk 2 cm into its host (bottom = host top - 0.02) standing at least 1.5 cm above the highest same-facing face it crosses (the host top, or the rib top where ribs run under it); a piece a rib runs into swallows it, so plates stand 5 cm proud (1.5 cm over their 3.5 cm beads), beads, nosings and the upper tread 3.5 cm over their host, and the two rubber mats are boxes y host top - 0.02..rib top + 0.015 with decals as flat quads at that same height (a decal on a mat 1.5 cm higher). Check: audit 0 FLICKER / 0 EDGE on `Interior/Shell/Floor/*`; fallback: sink the piece 1 cm deeper or raise it 1 cm.
- **The rib** (D11, D16, D19): a trapezoid prism 10 cm base, 6 cm top, 2 cm high on a 0.25 m pitch; across x the centres are ±(0.125 + 0.25k), k 0..12 = 26 ridges, outermost base edge 3.175, 6.5 cm inside the wall lip's inner face 3.24. Rib top = host top + 0.02. A rib is built as segments (start, end, any yaw), each ending 2 cm inside the piece that swallows it or 2 cm before any face it would otherwise meet (pocket wall, kick plate, nosing), never flush.
- **The wall lip** (phases 4 and 7): a channel strip x ±3.24..±3.36 against the wall, 5 cm tall from 2 cm under its host's top (3 cm proud), a half-cup scallop 6 cm across cut into its top inner edge every 0.25 m (photo 1); built by `van_floor_sheet.gd`.
