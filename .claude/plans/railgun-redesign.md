- (1) Two parallel rails run to one flush tip with open space between them; (2) the rear end, the first thing the player sees, is a boxy panelled receiver, no triangle wedge; (3) it still reads as a rusted scavenged weapon (rust, tape, scorch visible), a long two-hander held in one big hand; (4) the gun's top is flat: nothing sticks up taller than the rails (owner, D13), on the body only: boon pieces and sockets are out of scope and may stand taller; the look-judge is told so (from Scope). The run stops when any of the four fails.
