## travel_world
travel_world.gd (247 lines) is a RefCounted helper holding tc: Node (TravelController) and owns tile spawn/prune, variant picking, side streets, junction placement and act statues.

(1) Tiles: configure_initial_route (travel_world.gd:14-31) builds a straight Curve3D of route_length 10000 m, spawns one tile behind the van at +segment_length (20 m) with no route_progress meta, then spawn_route_segments_until(segment_ahead_distance=80). Each physics tick TravelController._physics_process step (travel_controller.gd:382) calls spawn_segments_ahead, which spawns tiles while _next_segment_progress <= van progress + 80, stepping by segment_length 20 (travel_world.gd:40-47). The tile's route_progress is tc._next_segment_progress, stored as meta "route_progress" plus "route_gen" (travel_world.gd:55-56); the transform is sample_route_transform(progress) (curve.sample_baked_with_rotation). prune_world (travel_world.gd:233-246, called at travel_controller.gd:385) frees any _world_pieces entry farther than world_cull_distance 140 m from the van rig, skipping _active_stop and _active_statue, and does nothing while _turn_state != NONE. So roughly 80/20 = 4-5 tiles ahead + up to 7 behind (140/20), about 11-12 alive. spawn_segments_ahead is also called after a turn (travel_routes.gd:97) and after leaving a stop (travel_routes.gd:263); attach_stop_on_upcoming_segment (travel_stops.gd:44-53) may spawn a host tile directly via spawn_world_segment.

(2) Variant: spawn_world_segment calls segment.apply_variant(pick_segment_variant()) (travel_world.gd:57-58). pick_segment_variant (118-132) is a "neighborhood" run-length scheme: when _neighborhood_remaining hits 0 it draws a new variant tc._rng.randi() % SEGMENT_VARIANT_COUNT (4), bumps by 1 if equal to _last_neighborhood_variant, and draws a run length randi_range(NEIGHBORHOOD_MIN_LENGTH 2, NEIGHBORHOOD_MAX_LENGTH 5). Consecutive tiles share one variant for 2-5 tiles; no district/act concept beyond this. tc._rng is seeded from GameSession.run_seed (travel_controller.gd:142-143), so it IS seeded (the rules file claim that side-street placement is unseeded is stale: pick_side_streets also uses tc._rng). The same _rng also feeds stop picks, bay side and auto route choice, so any change to RNG call order shifts everything downstream.

(3) Side streets: pick_side_streets (135-158): none before _segment_index 4 (SIDE_STREET_START_SEGMENT), a cooldown of SIDE_STREET_GAP 2 tiles after each opening, then SIDE_STREET_CHANCE 0.26 per tile; on success randi_range(0,2) picks left / right / both. corridor_segment.apply_side_streets hides that side's wall+upper wall meshes, disables collision, shows $SideStreets/Left|Right (side_street_branch.tscn instances at x=-9/+9, right one mirror_x), hides the variant's side-tagged structure nodes by name prefix (corridor_segment.gd:145-171), and rebuilds road openings/corner returns. Junctions are not per-tile: spawn_approaching_junction (204-208) picks t_junction_scene or crossroads_scene by GameSession.uses_t_junction() (game_session.gd:266) on the ROUTE_CHOICE phase (travel_controller.gd:445-447); spawn_special_ahead (184-201) aligns it to the tile lattice at about van progress + junction_distance 100 (aligned_special_progress 169-181), spawns tiles up to the final approach segment, pauses tile spawning, and adds the junction to _world_pieces. Both junction scenes share corridor_t_junction.gd (corner returns/sidewalk trims only, 63 lines).

(4) Statues: spawn_act_statue (211-223) instances act_statue_scene (Pedestal/Figure/Orb primitives, no script) at van_rig position + basis*(4.5, 0, -8) (right of and 8 m ahead of the van), same basis as the rig, appended to _world_pieces and exempt from pruning until clear_act_statue (226-230). Driven by begin_act_statue_stop/end_act_statue_stop (travel_controller.gd:310-317) from act_deck_controller.gd:70-136.

(5) Theming: none. Nothing in scripts/travel or scenes/corridor/*.gd reads run_act, card kind, BLESSING/DANGER or a theme; the only GameSession input to the world is uses_t_junction(). Walls are one ShaderMaterial (industrial_surface.gdshader, base_color 0.16/0.18/0.17, tile_count 14x2) on a 0.4x20x20 BoxMesh at x=±9 with an upper wall stacked at y≈20 (corridor_segment.tscn:8-22, 121-135).

(6) Outside call sites on corridor_segment: apply_variant travel_world.gd:57-58; apply_side_streets travel_world.gd:59-61 and travel_stops.gd:64-65 (cleared to false,false before opening a bay); open_bay travel_stops.gd:66-67; open_shop_bay travel_stops.gd:68-69 (fallback, never reached since open_bay exists); set_carriageway_visible stop_elevator.gd:81-83 (true, restore_road), :247-249 and :264-266 (false, _hide_overlapping_roads). No other methods on the tile are called from outside; metas route_progress/route_gen are read by travel_world.gd:74,94,104-108. Garage bays: the stop host tile is chosen 1-2 tiles ahead after finish_turn (travel_routes.gd:93-96), its side street is stripped and the bay opened on tc._stop_bay_side.

## street_shaders
Street surfaces are two hand-written spatial shaders. asphalt_surface.gdshader (228 lines) samples in WORLD XZ (v_world_pos from MODEL_MATRIX in vertex()), so tiling is implicit and independent of UV/BoxMesh size; it uses sin-dot hash21/hash22, smoothstep-eased value noise21, a 4-octave fbm (gain 0.5, lacunarity 2.07, offset (17.3,9.1)), and a Worldy "crack_field" that returns second-minus-first neighbour distance over a 3x3 cell search. Feature layers: grit fbm(pos*4.5), micro grain noise21(pos*28), three Gaussian tire lanes at x=±1.4 (width 0.7) and centre, oil blotches from two fbm thresholds, rotated/stretched elliptical repair patches per 3.2x3.8 m cell (62% roll), cracks masked to ~40% coverage plus hairlines, edge dirt from |x| 4.5..7.5, and per-1.35 m-cell litter (paper/can/plastic/debris, 22% at trash=1). Colours are mixed in order onto base, then grain multiply, then a washout bleach toward vec3(0.16,0.155,0.145) and a luma desaturate. Outputs METALLIC (0.02 + oil*0.18 + can*0.35), ROUGHNESS clamped 0.3..1.0 from roughness_value plus per-feature offsets, and a fake NORMAL_MAP built from grit/fbm/crack/litter deltas with NORMAL_MAP_DEPTH 0.75. sidewalk_surface.gdshader (125 lines) is UV-based instead: pos=(UV-0.5)*surface_size_m, uv_m=UV*surface_size_m; same hash/noise/fbm/crack_field helpers (crack_field uses hash21 twice, no hash22). Layers: slab seams every slab_spacing_m along UV.y plus cross seams at UV.x extremes, foot-traffic aisle wear, stains, cracks weighted by seams, wall/curb dirt at UV.x extremes; METALLIC 0, ROUGHNESS clamped 0.4..1.0, NORMAL_MAP_DEPTH 0.4. road_floor_materials.gd has three static factories (asphalt_mat, sidewalk_mat, std) that load() the shader and create a NEW ShaderMaterial per call with no caching; road_floor.gd calls them once per _build(). Detail props are all BoxMesh MeshInstance3D via RoadFloor._add_box_centered_to (material_override, shadows on, optional BoxShape3D collision under a StaticBody3D), with a per-builder RandomNumberGenerator seeded by road._seed_value(salt) (detail_seed ^ salt, else hash(Vector3(span_x, span_z, salt))). Building walls today are corridor_segment.tscn BoxMesh sub-resources sharing one "WallMaterial" ShaderMaterial on the much simpler industrial_surface.gdshader (UV tile grid, single hash, flat METALLIC 0.58). Environment lives in van.tscn as an inline sub_resource; no sky, glow, tonemap, SSAO or volumetric fog is set.

FACTS:
 - asphalt shader maps in world XZ (no UV, no tiling param); sidewalk shader maps in UV scaled by surface_size_m uniform

PITFALLS:
 - Facades will be lit almost entirely by the 0.45-energy directional plus 0.24 ambient and the depth fog that reaches full colour at 56 m; the fog colour is nearly black, so bright facade albedos read only near the van and detail past ~20 m fades. Emissive windows/signs are the only way to read far facades.
 - The asphalt shader uses world-space XZ; a wall shader must use world-space XY or YZ (or object-space) chosen per face normal, or a triplanar blend, because BoxMesh side UVs are 0..1 per face and the walls are 20 m long, so UV-based noise will stretch.
 - industrial_surface.gdshader's WallMaterial is one shared sub_resource across LeftWall, RightWall, upper walls and all BuildingBlock meshes in corridor_segment.tscn, and the same shader is preloaded by five stop scripts; changing that shader changes shops/garages/vestibules too. Add a new facade shader rather than editing it.
 - Bay walls open by hiding LeftWall/RightWall (and upper copies) and prop nodes matched by name prefix in corridor_segment.gd _is_side_structure_node; any new facade prop placed on a side must follow those name prefixes (Left*/Right*, RibL*/RibR*, etc.) or be built under a node the opening code hides, or it will cover a garage/shop entrance.
 - Variant props in corridor_segment.tscn are hand-placed transforms, not procedural; each Variant0..3 node holds ~20-30 MeshInstance3D children. A procedural facade builder should be a RefCounted helper like road_floor_details.gd with seeded RNG, not more hand-placed nodes.
 - road_floor_materials factories create a new ShaderMaterial per call (one per tile per material); a facade material with many uniforms should either be cached per variant or kept cheap, since tiles are spawned and culled continuously.
 - Threaded loading of van.tscn already dies on the floor shader sub-resource (see rules): keep new shaders as separate .gdshader files loaded from scripts, not inline sub_resources in van.tscn.
 - travel_controller.gd is 603 lines and over cap on purpose; any new variant/facade selection state should go in travel_world.gd or a new helper, not the controller.
 - The smoke fingerprint and scene dump baselines can move if corridor_segment.tscn or van.tscn node trees change; bless only intended changes.
